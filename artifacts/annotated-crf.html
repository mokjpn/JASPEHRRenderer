Mapping intent and review boundaries

Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium (CDISC) Dataset Specialization targets: VS = Vital Signs; DM = Demographics; STUDYID = Study Identifier; SUBJID = Subject Identifier for the Study; USUBJID = Unique Subject Identifier across studies; BRTHDTC = Date/Time of Birth; SEX = Sex; PULSE = Pulse Rate; DIABP = diastolic blood pressure; VSORRES = original result; VSTESTCD = vital signs test short name; SYSBP = systolic blood pressure; AGE = age.

Derived is this demo’s intent. The official AGE specialization declares Collected. This demo does not claim full specialization compliance, execute derivations, or generate an SDTM dataset.

Derived direction

birth-date [birthDate] + reference-date [referenceDate] → DM.AGE (AGE specialization)

Intent: 生年月日と研究基準日から、基準日時点の年齢を年単位で導出する

Optional derivation description (inert text): referenceDateからbirthDateを差し引いた期間を、基準日時点の年齢として年単位で表す。満年齢か小数年齢か、部分日付・閏日・丸め等の扱いは下流仕様で明示的に決定する。

Unresolved downstream decisions: completed years versus fractional age; partial or missing dates; leap-day birthdays; timezone/date boundaries; invalid date order; rounding. No age is calculated in this demonstration.
